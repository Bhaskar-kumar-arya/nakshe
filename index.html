How the numbers hold up

A wrong quantity costs the shop money or the job. So the AI never does the maths.

  1. The AI only readsGemini turns the sketch into dimensions and Sarvam turns the voice note into text. Neither one calculates.
  2. The contractor confirmsThe plan is read back on WhatsApp before any price is shown. Anything missing, like a roof slab, is asked for.
  3. Rules do the countingIS 1200 take-off rules, unit-tested on known rooms. Every line shows how it was counted.
  4. The owner approvesNothing leaves the shop without one tap, and the owner can edit any rate first.

Built with

Five of the six are Hack Sprint partner tools.

  • WhatsAppChannelWhere contractors already send photos and voice notes. Nothing to install.
  • n8nPartnerRuns the loop: receive, understand, confirm, estimate, approve, send.
  • Google GeminiPartnerReads the sketch and site photo into dimensions, each with a confidence score.
  • Sarvam AIPartnerKannada voice note to text, and the spoken quote back.
  • PaytmPartnerAdvance link inside the quote; payment holds the stock for 48 hours.
  • Trugen AIPartner“Talk to the shop”: a live video assistant that explains and re-prices the quote.
